Finance Vault Dashboard

A private, local-first personal finance dashboard for account balance and transaction tracking.

Purpose

Finance Vault Dashboard is used by the account owner to view personal financial accounts in one place, track current balances, and review transaction history for personal net worth and cash flow tracking.

Data Access

When the account owner connects an institution, the application may request access to account names, account balances, account types, and transaction history needed for the dashboard.

  • Balances are used to update personal net worth views.
  • Transactions are used for account review and activity tracking.
  • Account details are used to match connected accounts to the dashboard.

Privacy And Storage

Finance Vault Dashboard is designed as a private local application. Financial data is stored in an encrypted vault on the account owner's machine. The app does not sell personal financial data and does not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.
